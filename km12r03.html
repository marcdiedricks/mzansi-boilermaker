Team Dynamics

Curriculum-aligned learning support for workplace relationships and performance.

← KM-12 lessons
Learning support only. This module develops workplace judgement, communication and performance awareness. It does not diagnose health, personality or competence, and it does not replace workplace supervision, HR processes or professional support.

1. Working as a team

A team coordinates people around a shared purpose. Useful teams make expectations, roles and ways of working clear.

2. Team norms and ground rules

Ground rules can help teams agree how they communicate, make decisions, manage disagreements and share responsibilities.

3. Team-player styles

People may contribute in different ways, such as organising, analysing, supporting, generating ideas or completing tasks. Effective teams use these differences constructively.

4. Stages of team development

Teams often move through stages as members get to know the work, experience disagreement, establish working norms and improve performance.

5. Trust and communication

Trust grows when people communicate clearly, keep commitments, share relevant information and treat one another respectfully.

Key idea: Strong teams are built through repeated reliable behaviour, not slogans.
