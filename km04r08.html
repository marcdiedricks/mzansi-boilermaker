Pipe Work Characteristics

Build recognition of pipe and flange specifications, component categories and identification systems while keeping current standards and workplace information as the authority.

← KM-04 lessons
Knowledge learning only. This lesson explains concepts, terminology, drawings, hazards and controlled information. It does not provide step-by-step instructions for cutting, welding, heating, bending, lifting, machine operation or other hazardous practical work. Practical activities require approved training and competent human supervision.

1. Pipe specifications

Pipe systems are defined by controlled specifications that may address dimensions, material, service and other requirements. Learners should understand the purpose of specifications without assuming one value applies universally.

2. Flange specifications

Flanges are selected and identified according to the system and governing specification. Visual similarity alone does not prove interchangeability or suitability.

3. Pipe components

Common systems contain different component types serving different functions. The learner should recognise broad categories and know that exact selection belongs to controlled design and workplace processes.

4. Colour and identification systems

Colour coding may be used as one part of identification, but colour alone must not be treated as proof of contents, material or safety status. Current site rules, labels and controlled references must be checked.

5. Safe interpretation

Before relying on any pipe identification, confirm the applicable drawing, specification and workplace controls. This lesson does not provide live-system identification or intervention instructions.
