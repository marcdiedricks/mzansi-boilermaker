PM-01 • PM-01-PS03 • PREPARATION

Draw and Interpret Simple Plate, Pipe and Structural Steel Drawings

Safe preparation and evidence support for a formal practical skill activity.

← PM-01 activities
Human-supervised practical boundary. This page does not certify practical competence and does not provide operating instructions for machinery, cutting, welding, forming or other hazardous work. Real practical activity must take place under the approved provider/workplace process and competent adult supervision.

1. Classroom drawing practice

Use paper or approved digital tools to practise simple views, dimensions, labels and drawing organisation without turning the exercise into an uncontrolled fabrication instruction.

2. Interpretation

Explain what each view represents, what dimensions control, and which information would still require clarification.

3. Revision discipline

Label practice work clearly as training material and keep it separate from approved workplace drawings.

Key idea: Drawing practice develops communication skill; approval remains a separate human-controlled process.

Evidence preparation

Where your provider or workplace permits it, prepare evidence such as annotated classroom drawings, calculations, material lists, sketches, completed worksheets or photographs of supervised work. Evidence uploaded to the PWA remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
