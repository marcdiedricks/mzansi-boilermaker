Basic Plate, Pipe and Structural Steel Drawings

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Boilermaker’s Drawings.

← KM-07 lessons
Drawing and theory learning only. This lesson develops interpretation, geometry, measurement and drawing literacy. It does not authorise fabrication, cutting, welding, lifting, machining or any other hazardous practical work.

1. Plate drawings

Plate drawings can communicate shape, dimensions, openings, edges, interfaces and other controlled information relevant to a component.

2. Pipe drawings

Pipe-related drawings can show component relationships, orientation, dimensions and identifiers. Learners should read them as controlled information rather than infer details from appearance.

3. Structural steel drawings

Structural drawings can show members, locations, interfaces and dimensions within a larger assembly.

4. Cross-checking information

Learners should compare views, dimensions, notes and identifiers to build a consistent understanding. Conflicts must be referred for clarification.

Key idea: A drawing should tell one consistent technical story.
