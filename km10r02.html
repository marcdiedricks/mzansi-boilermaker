Software Used to Communicate Information

Curriculum-aligned learning support for basic computers, communication software, digital responsibility, 4IR and design thinking.

← KM-10 lessons
Learning support only. This module develops basic digital literacy for the occupational environment. Workplace IT policies, privacy rules, cybersecurity controls and authorised software usage remain binding.

1. Electronic text documents

Word-processing software is used to create, edit and organise written information such as notes, reports and instructions.

2. Electronic spreadsheets

Spreadsheet software can organise data in rows and columns and support simple calculations, lists and record keeping.

3. Internet and written communication

Internet access can support research and communication, while email and other approved written channels provide a record of messages and documents.

4. Electronic meetings and seminars

Video meetings and webinars allow people to communicate and learn remotely. Learners should understand basic meeting etiquette and use only approved platforms and links.

5. Mobile technology

Phones and tablets can access documents, learning materials and communication systems. Small screens and mobile data limits make clear, low-data content especially important.

Key idea: Choose the digital tool that fits the task and workplace policy.
