Dimensioning

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Boilermaker’s Drawings.

← KM-07 lessons
Drawing and theory learning only. This lesson develops interpretation, geometry, measurement and drawing literacy. It does not authorise fabrication, cutting, welding, lifting, machining or any other hazardous practical work.

1. What dimensions communicate

Dimensions state controlled size, location and relationship information for features on a drawing.

2. Read before calculating

Learners should identify which dimensions are given, which relationships are implied, and which values must not be assumed.

3. Tolerances and notes

Where tolerances or special notes are shown, they form part of the requirement. Learners should understand their purpose without inventing missing limits.

4. Avoid scaling from the picture

The drawn image is a communication aid; the stated dimensional information is the controlled requirement.

Key idea: Never replace a stated dimension with an estimate from the image.
