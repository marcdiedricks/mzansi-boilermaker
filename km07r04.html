Line Types and Their Uses

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Boilermaker’s Drawings.

← KM-07 lessons
Drawing and theory learning only. This lesson develops interpretation, geometry, measurement and drawing literacy. It does not authorise fabrication, cutting, welding, lifting, machining or any other hazardous practical work.

1. Why line types matter

Different line styles can communicate different kinds of drawing information. Learners should recognise that line meaning depends on the drawing convention being used.

2. Visible and hidden information

Some lines indicate visible outlines while others may represent features not directly visible in the chosen view. Their interpretation helps build a mental picture of the component.

3. Centre and reference information

Certain line types can identify centres, axes or other reference information used to understand geometry.

4. Controlled convention

The exact convention must come from the current drawing standard or approved training material. The app does not invent line meanings.

Key idea: Interpret line types from the controlled convention, not personal preference.
