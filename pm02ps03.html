Maintain Stationary Machinery — Condition Awareness

Safe preparation, recognition and evidence support for supervised practical training.

← PM-02 activities
High-risk practical boundary. This page does not teach tool sharpening, machine maintenance procedures, powered-tool operation, access-equipment operation, welding/cutting equipment servicing or machinery operation. Those activities require approved equipment, workplace procedures and competent adult supervision.

1. Recognise machinery condition information

Practise reading machine identity, inspection status, warning labels and training checklists without changing settings or attempting maintenance.

2. Defect reporting

Visible damage, abnormal condition, missing guarding or unclear inspection status should be reported through the approved process.

3. Machine-maintenance boundary

Adjustment, blade or die work, clearance checks, repairs and test runs can create serious hazards and must remain in supervised practical training.

Key idea: Do not troubleshoot stationary machinery through trial and error.

Evidence preparation

Where permitted by the provider or workplace, prepare non-operational evidence such as completed inspection checklists, labelled tool-identification worksheets, defect reports, maintenance records supplied for training, photographs of supervised activities, and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
