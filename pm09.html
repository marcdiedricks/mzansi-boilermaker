PRACTICAL SKILLS • PM-09

Sling, Lift and Secure Loads for Transportation

High-risk lifting module. The official detailed curriculum lists seven practical skill activities. This PWA supports theory preparation, hazard recognition, controlled-document awareness, classroom calculations and evidence organisation only. Real lifting, slinging, crane direction, load movement and securing remain supervised practical work.
