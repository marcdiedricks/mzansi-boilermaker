## PURPOSE

This is the controlled visual-learning reference library for the Mzansi Boilermaker PWA.

The offline written lesson remains complete and usable without internet. External visual material is supplementary only and must never be cached into the PWA, required for learner progress, used as proof of competence, or used to authorise practical work.

For hazardous topics, acceptable visual support is limited to theory, recognition, safety awareness, drawings, inspection, quality and process overview. Operational tutorials that could enable unsupervised hazardous work are not approved.

- VETTING REQUIRED — no learner-facing link may be published yet.

## HIGH-RISK FILTER

For KM-06 and KM-14, and for any future PM/WM visual reference touching welding, cutting, machinery, forming, lifting, rigging, pressure systems or other hazardous trade work:

- do not approve step-by-step operating tutorials;
- do not approve settings, rigging configurations, lifting methods, hand-signal sequences, gas/electrical setup, machine setup or similar operational instruction;
- prefer diagrams, animations, hazard awareness, process overview, defect recognition, inspection concepts, drawing interpretation and quality awareness;
- route real practical work to the approved provider/workplace process and competent human supervision.
