Erect and Assemble Pipes and Steel Structures — Preparation

Safe preparation and evidence support for supervised practical training.

← PM-05 activities
High-risk practical boundary. Erection, lifting, slinging, positioning, mechanical joining, welding and site preparation can create crush, fall, suspended-load and machinery hazards. This page does not provide rigging configurations, hand-signal sequences, lifting procedures, welding instructions, erection sequences or live site methods. Real practical work requires approved procedures and competent adult supervision.

1. Understand the task

Read drawings, sequence information, component IDs, interfaces and inspection points while keeping real erection and assembly within supervised workplace training.

Use the approved drawing, task brief, inspection record or site instruction supplied by the provider/workplace. Separate stated requirements from assumptions and flag uncertainty before work begins.

2. Hazard and authority awareness

Recognise broad risks such as suspended loads, unstable components, pinch/crush zones, work at height, access restrictions, hot work, lifting equipment and changing site conditions. Do not enter or control a live practical operation unless formally authorised and supervised.

3. Quality and compliance awareness

Learn how drawings, tolerances, specifications, component identity and inspection criteria guide human checks. The PWA can help organise observations but cannot accept or certify a structure.

4. Evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare non-operational evidence such as annotated drawings, classroom weight calculations, checklists, component-identification sheets, supervised-work photographs and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
