Securing Worksites — Hazard, Signage and Evacuation Awareness

Curriculum-aligned theory support for workplace fundamentals and workplace safety.

← KM-11 lessons
Safety-awareness learning only. This lesson does not provide emergency-response certification, first-aid certification, fire-fighting authorisation or permission to secure a hazardous worksite. In a real emergency, follow site procedures, alert trained adults/supervisors and use authorised emergency services.

1. Hazards and risks

A hazard is something that can cause harm, while risk considers the likelihood and seriousness of that harm in a particular situation.

2. Risk-control principles

Workplace safety processes generally involve identifying hazards, assessing risks and applying appropriate controls. Learners should understand these principles without independently authorising high-risk work.

3. Safety signage and barriers

Safety signs, colour codes and barriers communicate warnings, restrictions and required behaviour. Their exact meaning must be learned from the approved site and training system.

4. Reporting emergencies

Emergency situations should be reported promptly through the site's approved channels. Learners should know who to alert and where emergency information is displayed.

5. Evacuation

Evacuation procedures define routes, assembly points and responsibilities. Learners should follow site instructions and supervisors rather than inventing an alternative route unless an immediate danger makes the designated route unusable.

Key idea: Securing a high-risk worksite is an authorised workplace responsibility, not an app task.
