- offline-first and low-data design rules
- build-small, test, verify and freeze governance

VISUAL LEARNING CONTROL:

Videos must not authorise hazardous practical work, replace supervision, or be treated as evidence of competence, assessment or certification. Prefer South African, relevant, low-data and appropriate-language resources where practical. Video links must be checked for relevance and availability during build and testing.

- support learning and revision
- provide plain-language explanations
- organise learning into small progressive units
- support multilingual explanation where practical
- support offline learning
- support low-data use
- record learner progress

- claim SAQA approval

- claim SABS approval
- claim SANS certification
- act as an assessor
- act as a competent person
- authorise hazardous practical work
- declare occupational competence
- issue trade-test results
- issue qualifications or certificates
- replace an accredited provider, workplace supervisor, assessor, AQP or formal trade-test process

Frozen wording:

> Build in alignment with applicable South African occupational qualifications, curriculum documents, legislation, regulatory authorities and technical standards. # 10. EXIT-LEVEL OUTCOME CONTROL

The current qualification requires learners to demonstrate integrated outcomes in these broad areas:

1. Interpret plating and structural steel drawings and technical documents, develop patterns/templates and mark off materials.
2. Fabricate components and apply legal and industry standards to inspection and quality.
3. Manipulate and fabricate profiles and metal sections using appropriate forming principles.
4. Assemble plate and pipe components into structures and apply design specifications and industry standards.

The PWA may teach and prepare learners for these outcomes.

The PWA must not declare that an exit-level outcome has been formally achieved.

# 14. TECHNICAL-STANDARDS CONTROL

## 14.1 Fundamental rule

There is no single generic SANS list that can safely be attached to every Boilermaker lesson.

Technical standards must be mapped by:

CURRICULUM MODULE  
→ LEARNING / WORK ACTIVITY  
→ HAZARD / QUALITY AREA  
→ APPLICABLE LEGISLATION  
→ APPLICABLE TECHNICAL STANDARD  
→ VERIFIED EDITION  
→ SOURCE / PROVENANCE  
→ REVIEW DATE

## 14.2 SANS 347

Control status:
MODULE-SPECIFIC — PRESSURE EQUIPMENT

Use:
Hazard categorisation / conformity-assessment framework for pressure equipment where applicable under South African regulation.

Important:
The current edition/status must be verified from an authoritative SABS/SANS source before a lesson or compliance reference is released.

The full copyrighted text must not be copied into this repository unless appropriately licensed.

Government regulatory relevance source:
https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201711/41256gon1265.pdf

## 14.3 Incorporated pressure-equipment standards

The Pressure Equipment Regulations may incorporate additional health-and-safety standards for design, manufacture, inspection, repair, testing and related pressure-equipment activities.

Control:
Each incorporated standard must be verified against the current government incorporation notice and current SABS/SANS status before use.

## 14.4 Driven machinery and lifting standards

Modules involving lifting, slinging, machinery, load securing or associated machinery must be checked against:

- Occupational Health and Safety Act
- Driven Machinery Regulations
- current incorporated health-and-safety standards
- applicable workplace procedures
- manufacturer documentation where equipment-specific

This applies especially to KM-14 and PM-09.

## 14.5 Welding, cutting and hot-work standards

Modules KM-06, PM-03 and WM-03 require controlled mapping to:

- applicable OHS legislation
- General Safety Regulations
- relevant welding/cutting quality standards
- relevant workplace procedures
- manufacturer specifications
- client/site specifications where applicable

The app may explain principles, terminology, hazards, inspection concepts and quality requirements.

It must not provide unsupervised operational instructions for hazardous work.

## 14.6 Fabrication and structural-quality standards

Modules KM-04, KM-05, PM-04, PM-05, WM-04 and WM-05 require standards mapping where work depends on:

- structural specifications
- material specifications
- dimensional tolerances
- joining requirements
- inspection requirements
- fabrication drawings
- quality plans
- applicable codes

The controlling standard depends on the actual component, workplace, client specification and regulated environment.

## 14.7 Drawing standards

KM-07 and PM-01 must use controlled, current conventions for:

- engineering drawing terminology
- views
- dimensions
- symbols
- abbreviations
- tolerances
- material identification
- fabrication documentation

Any specific SANS/ISO drawing standard used in curriculum content must be entered into the Standard Register before publication.

# 16. COPYRIGHT AND STANDARDS LICENSING RULE

SANS/SABS and other technical standards may be copyright-protected.

Therefore:

- do not reproduce complete proprietary standards in GitHub without permission/licensing
- do not paste complete standards into lessons
- record standard number, title, verified edition and applicability
- summarise only the requirements needed for learning within lawful limits
- link to the authoritative source where appropriate
- preserve provenance
- record licensing status
- do not imply ownership of SABS/SANS material

Public legislation, public qualification records and official curriculum metadata may be cited and mapped as authoritative sources, subject to their stated terms.

# 19. SAFETY ARCHITECTURE

Safety-critical authorisation must never be delegated to a generative AI response.

Use deterministic software rules.

## Tier 1 — Theory

Examples:

- terminology
- drawing interpretation
- safety theory
- materials concepts
- quality concepts

AI teaching permitted.

## Tier 2 — Controlled low-risk learning practice

Only where the task is genuinely low-risk and permitted by local training rules.

## Tier 3 — Higher-risk practical activity

Human supervision required.

Examples include curriculum areas involving powered machinery, hot work, lifting, forming, fabrication or similar higher-risk practical environments.

The app may provide learning support but must not authorise the physical activity.

## Tier 4 — Restricted / critical hazard

The PWA must deny operational authorisation and route the learner to competent human supervision and the applicable workplace procedure.

This includes regulated or critical-hazard contexts such as pressure-equipment work and other tasks where law, workplace controls or competent-person requirements apply.

# 34. COMPLETENESS RULE

This master file is intended to be comprehensive in control scope.

However, it must not pretend that every technical standard applicable to every possible Boilermaker workplace, client, pressure system, welding procedure, lifting system, material, fabrication code or site has already been permanently identified.

The correct quality rule is:

1. maintain the complete qualification and curriculum structure here
2. maintain the complete authority and legislation framework here
3. maintain all verified standards in the Standard Register
4. add additional standards only after authoritative verification
5. map standards to the exact curriculum/work activity
6. never guess a standard number, title or edition
7. never use a superseded standard silently
8. never reproduce proprietary standards unlawfully
9. block authoritative publication where a required standard is still unverified

This protects both veracity and maintainability.
