PRACTICAL SKILLS • PM-06

Work Safely and Respond to Emergencies

Emergency-awareness module. The official curriculum contains two practical skill activities: identifying potential hazards/responding to emergencies, and implementing evacuation procedures. This PWA supports recognition, reporting, simulated checklist work and evacuation awareness only. Real emergency action remains governed by site procedures and trained responders.
