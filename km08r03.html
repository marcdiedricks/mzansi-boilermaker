Principles of Ethics

Curriculum-aligned learning support for environmental awareness, energy efficiency and ethics.

← KM-08 lessons
Learning support only. This lesson develops workplace awareness and responsible decision-making. It does not replace employer policies, environmental procedures, legislation, supervision or formal assessment.

1. What ethics means

Ethics concerns principles used to judge what is responsible, fair and acceptable behaviour. In the workplace, ethical conduct supports trust, safety, quality and respectful relationships.

2. Ethical and non-ethical behaviour

Ethical behaviour includes honesty, accountability, respect for others, responsible use of company resources and truthful reporting. Non-ethical behaviour can include hiding mistakes, falsifying records, unfair treatment or knowingly ignoring important rules.

3. Ethical decisions in technical work

A learner may face pressure to save time, hide a defect, use unverified information or ignore a concern. Ethical conduct means following the approved process, reporting accurately and refusing to misrepresent work or evidence.

4. Responsibility and sustainability

Ethical behaviour also includes considering the effects of work on other people, the workplace and the environment. Responsible choices should align with legal duties, workplace rules and professional expectations.

Key idea: Doing the right thing includes reporting truthfully even when a mistake is inconvenient.
