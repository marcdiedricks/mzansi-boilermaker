Shift Loads on an Inclination — Planning Awareness

High-risk practical preparation and evidence support.

← PM-09 activities
High-risk lifting boundary. This page does not provide sling-selection procedures, rigging configurations, knot instructions, load-control methods, lifting sequences, crane hand signals, equipment setup, incline-moving methods or physical inspection procedures. Real lifting work requires approved equipment, site procedures and competent adult supervision.

1. Understand the activity

Recognise why incline, surface condition, verified load information, route control, equipment status and authorised planning matter. No incline-moving technique is taught here.

2. Information and planning awareness

At preparation level, identify the controlled drawing or task brief, verified load information, authorised lifting plan, environmental conditions, exclusion requirements and responsible people. If any of these are unclear, stop and escalate rather than guessing.

3. Hazard and authority awareness

Recognise broad risks such as suspended loads, unstable loads, crush zones, changing ground conditions, poor visibility, damaged equipment and conflicting communication. Learners must not direct, rig or move a real load without the required training and authorisation.

4. Evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare non-operational evidence such as classroom weight calculations using supplied data, annotated lifting-plan examples, hazard-identification worksheets, equipment-identification sheets, simulated checklists and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
