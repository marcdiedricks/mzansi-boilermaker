Recognise Quality Requirements Before Fabrication

A short quality-awareness lesson within KM-04 Fabrication and Pipework.

← Main app
Learning only. This lesson supports recognition of quality requirements in controlled job information. It does not teach cutting, welding, heating, bending, lifting, machine setup, machine operation or physical inspection procedures. Practical work requires approved training, workplace controls and competent human supervision.

1. Quality starts with controlled job information

Before fabrication, the learner should understand what the current approved drawing, specification, job card or other controlled workplace information requires. Quality is not based on what looks approximately right.

Key idea: The requirement for the job must come from controlled information, not memory or appearance alone.

2. Identify what must be checked

Job information may specify material, dimensions, quantity, orientation, identification, finish or other acceptance requirements. The learner should recognise which requirements apply before practical work begins.

3. Use the correct document revision

A superseded or unclear document can lead to the wrong result even when work appears neat. Confirm that the information being used is the current approved information for the job.

4. Do not hide or guess around a mismatch

If material, dimensions, identification or other controlled information does not match, stop and report the mismatch through the workplace process. Do not invent a correction or continue on assumption.

5. Know the learning boundary

Recognising a quality requirement is not the same as carrying out or approving a fabrication inspection. Formal acceptance, practical decisions and authorisation remain with appropriately responsible people and workplace processes.
