WM-01 • WM-01-WE05 • WORKPLACE EVIDENCE

Lay Out and Produce Templates or Jigs from Approved Drawings

Evidence organisation and reflection for supervised workplace or approved simulated experience.

← WM-01 competencies
Evidence support only. This page does not create workplace experience, certify competence or authorise practical work. Hazardous activity must remain under approved provider/workplace controls and competent adult supervision.

1. Curriculum evidence focus

Prepare evidence references, approved drawing identifiers, photos of supervised work where permitted, and human sign-off records. Physical cutting or fabrication remains supervised.

2. Before recording evidence

Confirm the activity was authorised, supervised and linked to an approved workplace or provider task. Record the correct module/competency code, date, context and the responsible human reviewer. Do not create a new practical task merely to obtain evidence.

3. Suitable evidence categories

Depending on the provider or workplace rules, evidence may include job cards, approved drawings, checklists, inspection or quality records, logbook entries, permitted photographs, reports, attendance or supervisor observations. Sensitive or confidential workplace information should not be captured unless authorised.

4. Human verification

A learner may organise or upload evidence, but the app must keep it marked NOT VERIFIED until an authorised workplace supervisor, facilitator, assessor or other responsible human reviews it. App completion never equals workplace competence.
