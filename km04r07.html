Simple Pipe Work Component Fabrication

Recognise simple pipework components, terminology, drawings, specifications and hazards without providing practical fabrication instructions.

← KM-04 lessons
Knowledge learning only. This lesson explains concepts, terminology, drawings, hazards and controlled information. It does not provide step-by-step instructions for cutting, welding, heating, bending, lifting, machine operation or other hazardous practical work. Practical activities require approved training and competent human supervision.

1. Pipework components

Pipe systems can include pipe sections, fittings, flanges and other components selected to suit a defined service and specification. The learner should recognise component categories and their broad purpose.

2. Terminology, codes and symbols

Pipework drawings and specifications use controlled terminology and symbols. Meaning should be verified against the governing drawing, specification and approved references.

3. Drawings and specifications

The learner should be able to identify where size, component type, orientation and other job requirements are communicated. Missing or conflicting information must be referred for clarification.

4. Hazards and risks

Pipework fabrication may involve heavy components, sharp edges, pressure-related contexts, hot work and powered equipment. Theory learning must remain separate from authorisation to perform physical work.

5. Standards and boundaries

Specific pipe and pressure requirements vary by service, material and regulated environment. The app must not invent a universal value or procedure where a controlled standard is required.
