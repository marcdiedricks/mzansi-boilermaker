Materials Used in Fabrication

Recognise common fabrication materials, their basic properties, identification systems, visible defects and safe material-handling decision points.

← KM-03 lessons
Learning only. This lesson develops material recognition and safe decision-making. It does not authorise lifting, cutting, heating, welding, forming or other practical fabrication work.

1. Common material forms

Boilermaker fabrication can involve plates, tubes, pipes, rolled sections and hollow sections. These forms may look similar at a glance, but their dimensions, grade, specification and intended use can differ.

Key idea: Material must be identified from controlled information, not appearance alone.

2. Ferrous and non-ferrous materials

Ferrous materials contain iron as a main constituent. Non-ferrous materials do not use iron as the main constituent. At knowledge level, the learner should recognise that different metals have different physical properties and characteristics.

Material choice affects fabrication planning, quality requirements and the controls that apply.

3. Properties and characteristics matter

Materials can differ in strength, hardness, toughness, ductility, corrosion behaviour and other characteristics. These properties help determine whether a material is suitable for a particular controlled requirement.

This lesson does not teach how to test or process materials in practice.

4. Identification systems

Materials may be identified through labels, markings, certificates, colour codes, grade references, batch or heat numbers and other controlled systems used by the workplace or supplier.

If identification is missing, unclear or conflicting, stop and clarify through the responsible process.

5. Recognise visible material defects

Visible damage or defects can include distortion, cracking, corrosion, surface damage or other conditions that may require review. A learner should recognise that questionable material must not simply be accepted because it is available.

Final acceptance decisions belong to the approved workplace quality process and competent personnel.

6. Material handling needs controls

Plate, pipe, sections and other fabrication materials can be heavy, awkward, sharp-edged or unstable. Material handling therefore requires approved methods, suitable equipment, supervision and workplace controls.

This app does not provide lifting, rigging or handling instructions.
