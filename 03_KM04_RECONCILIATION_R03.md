## 2. AUTHORITATIVE KM-04 KNOWLEDGE TOPICS

The official KM-04 curriculum contains nine Knowledge Topics:

1. KM-04-KT01 — Basic Templates — 9%
2. KM-04-KT02 — Pipe and Plate Templates — 5%
3. KM-04-KT03 — Templates for Complex Components and Structures — 5%
4. KM-04-KT04 — Forming and Shaping Simple Components — 9%
5. KM-04-KT05 — Fabrication Using Various Structural Steel Shapes — 15%
6. KM-04-KT06 — Forming and Shaping Complex Components — 18%
7. KM-04-KT07 — Simple Pipe Work Component Fabrication — 9%
8. KM-04-KT08 — Pipe Work Characteristics — 15%
9. KM-04-KT09 — Marking Off Pipe Work — 15%

The final learner architecture therefore targets nine curriculum-aligned lessons.

---

## 4. CROSSWALK PRINCIPLE

The historical A–J blocks are cross-cutting preparation, quality, control and planning concepts. They should be embedded where relevant inside the nine official KM-04 topics rather than retained as ten separate curriculum lessons.

No useful pilot content is deleted merely to reduce lesson count. Content is consolidated, rewritten, or moved only where it improves curriculum alignment and learner simplicity.

## 6. FINAL NINE-LESSON KM-04 LEARNER ARCHITECTURE

### Lesson 1 — Basic Templates
Official basis: KM-04-KT01.
Must cover:
- types and techniques of templates;
- template materials;
- uses of templates;
- basic marking-off principles and techniques.
Reuse mainly from: A, C, G.

### Lesson 2 — Pipe and Plate Templates
Official basis: KM-04-KT02.
Must cover:
- pipe templates;
- plate templates;
- profile-cutter template use at knowledge level;
- interpreting relevant drawings;
- calculations used in laying out pipe and plate templates.
Reuse mainly from: B, E, G, H.

### Lesson 3 — Templates for Complex Components and Structures
Official basis: KM-04-KT03.
Must cover:
- complex-component templates;
- structural templates;
- CNC-related templates;
- drawing interpretation;
- related layout calculations.
Reuse mainly from: B, E, G, H.

### Lesson 4 — Forming and Shaping Simple Components
Official basis: KM-04-KT04.
Must cover:
- terminology, codes and symbols;
- simple components and applications;
- materials;
- hazards and safe-work principles;
- information found on drawings.
Reuse mainly from: B, C, D, F, I.

### Lesson 5 — Fabrication Using Structural Steel Shapes
Official basis: KM-04-KT05.
Must cover:
- structural-steel terminology;
- shapes, characteristics, applications and limitations;
- hazards and safe-work principles;
- codes/standards;
- materials;
- drawing symbols/abbreviations;
- fabrication procedure knowledge.
Reuse mainly from: B, C, D, F, I, J.

### Lesson 6 — Forming and Shaping Complex Components
Official basis: KM-04-KT06.
Must cover:
- types, characteristics and applications of complex components;
- drawings/specifications;
- relevant structures, components and steel shapes;
- hazards and safe-work principles.
Reuse mainly from: B, C, D, F, H, I.

### Lesson 7 — Simple Pipe Work Component Fabrication
Official basis: KM-04-KT07.
Must cover:
- pipework-component terminology;
- types, characteristics and applications;
- drawings/specifications;
- hazards, risks and safe-work principles;
- codes/standards/specifications.
Reuse mainly from: A, B, C, D, F, I, J.

### Lesson 8 — Pipe Work Characteristics
Official basis: KM-04-KT08.
Must cover:
- pipe and flange specifications;
- pipe colour coding;
- different pipe components.
Reuse mainly from: A, C, F, J.

### Lesson 9 — Marking Off Pipe Work
Official basis: KM-04-KT09.
Must cover:

- cutting-angle calculations.
Reuse mainly from: E, G, H.

## 7. GAPS THAT MUST BE BUILT, NOT ASSUMED

The existing A–J pilot content does not by itself prove full coverage of the following authoritative requirements:

- specific types and materials of templates;
- pipe and plate template distinctions;
- complex/structural/CNC template calculations;
- forming/shaping terminology, codes and symbols;
- structural-steel shapes, characteristics, applications and limitations;
- complex-component characteristics;
- pipe/flange specifications;
- pipe colour coding;
- contour marker and centre finder knowledge;
- cutting-angle calculations;
- all explicit Internal Assessment Criteria attached to KT01–KT09.

These are controlled content gaps for the final nine-lesson rebuild.

---

## 8. SAFETY / GOVERNANCE CONTROL

The official curriculum contains knowledge statements that reference procedures, machinery, forming, cutting, fabrication, hazards and pipework.

In this PWA:
- learner-facing lessons remain knowledge/theory support;
- hazardous practical work is not authorised;
- practical operation is not simulated as unsupervised instruction;
- videos are supplementary recognition/context resources only;
- quiz completion records learning progress only;
- formal assessment, competence and workplace authorisation remain outside the app.

---

## 9. ACCESSIBILITY CONTROL

Each final KM-04 lesson must remain:
- short enough for a phone session;
- complete offline in written form;
- plain-language first;
- low-data;
- visually simple;
- large-tap-target friendly;
- no more than one primary learning path;
- free of unnecessary build/governance language on the learner surface.

The authoritative complexity stays behind the learner interface.
