Identify Potential Hazards and Respond to Emergencies — Awareness

Safety-awareness preparation and evidence support for supervised practical training.

← PM-06 activities
Emergency-response boundary. This page teaches recognition, reporting, evacuation awareness and escalation only. It does not provide rescue procedures, fire-fighting methods, first-aid procedures, isolation sequences or other live emergency instructions. In a real emergency, follow the approved site process, alert trained adults/supervisors and use authorised emergency services.

1. Understand the activity

Recognise broad hazards and emergency categories in a simulated or supervised workplace, understand reporting/escalation channels, and know that emergency protocols must follow approved site procedures.

2. Recognition and reporting

Learn to identify broad hazard or emergency categories, know the site's reporting channels, and understand who has authority to coordinate the response. Do not enter a hazardous area or attempt a response beyond your training and authorisation.

3. Procedure awareness

Study the purpose of alarms, evacuation routes, assembly points, exclusion zones, checklists and emergency roles from approved training material. The actual site procedure remains the controlling instruction.

4. Evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare evidence such as completed hazard-identification worksheets, simulated emergency checklists, evacuation-map interpretation exercises, attendance records and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
