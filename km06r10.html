Laser Jet and Water Jet Cutting — Theory

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Cutting and Welding.

← KM-06 lessons
Theory only. Cutting, welding and heat-treatment processes involve serious hazards. This lesson explains concepts, process differences, quality, hazards and decision boundaries only. It does not provide operating sequences, machine settings, live-work instructions or authorisation. Practical training must be conducted in an approved environment under competent human supervision.

1. Two different cutting technologies

Laser cutting and water-jet cutting are distinct technologies used in appropriate applications. Learners should understand that they use different physical principles and equipment.

2. Application and limitations

Material type, thickness, edge-quality requirements, tolerances and equipment capability influence process selection. The correct choice belongs to the approved engineering and workplace process.

3. Automation and data control

These systems often depend on controlled digital files, machine configuration and production data. Revision control and verification are critical.

4. Safety boundary

Both technologies involve specialised high-energy equipment and serious hazards. Operation, maintenance and troubleshooting must be performed by trained and authorised personnel.

Key idea: The learning companion explains concepts only and never supplies operational machine instructions.
