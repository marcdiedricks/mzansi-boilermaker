KM-04 • LESSON 9 OF 9

Marking Off Pipe Work

Recognise the purpose, parts and controlled use context of contour markers and centre finders, and understand why calculations and practical marking-off require supervised training.

← KM-04 lessons
Knowledge learning only. This lesson explains concepts, terminology, drawings, hazards and controlled information. It does not provide step-by-step instructions for cutting, welding, heating, bending, lifting, machine operation or other hazardous practical work. Practical activities require approved training and competent human supervision.

1. Purpose of marking-off tools

The curriculum includes contour-marker and centre-finder applications. At knowledge level, learners should recognise that these tools support locating or representing reference information on pipework.

2. Tool identification

Learners should be able to identify the main parts or features of the relevant tools from approved training resources or manufacturer information. The exact tool design can vary.

3. Use and care at knowledge level

The safe learning objective is to understand purpose, inspection awareness, storage and the need to follow manufacturer and workplace instructions. This lesson does not teach practical tool setup or use.

4. Angles and calculations

The curriculum includes cutting-angle calculations. The learning companion may support mathematical understanding using approved examples, but physical transfer, cutting and fabrication remain supervised practical activities.

5. Verification before action

A calculated result must still be checked against the current drawing, specification, approved method and supervisor requirements before any practical work.
