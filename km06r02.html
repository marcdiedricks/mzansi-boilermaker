Heat Treatment

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Cutting and Welding.

← KM-06 lessons
Theory only. Cutting, welding and heat-treatment processes involve serious hazards. This lesson explains concepts, process differences, quality, hazards and decision boundaries only. It does not provide operating sequences, machine settings, live-work instructions or authorisation. Practical training must be conducted in an approved environment under competent human supervision.

1. Purpose of heat treatment

Heat treatment is used to influence material properties through controlled heating and cooling. Learners should understand the purpose and terminology without attempting to carry out a heat-treatment process.

2. Process control

Heat treatment depends on material type, approved procedure, required properties and quality controls. Exact temperatures, timing and treatment cycles belong to controlled workplace procedures and qualified supervision.

3. Potential effects

Heat treatment can alter hardness, toughness, ductility and residual stress. These effects explain why uncontrolled heating can damage a component or make it unsuitable for service.

4. Verification and documentation

Where heat treatment is required, records, material identity, procedure references and inspection evidence support traceability.

Key idea: The app teaches why controls matter; it does not supply operating parameters.
