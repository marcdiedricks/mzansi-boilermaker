Basics of Environmental Sustainability

Curriculum-aligned learning support for environmental awareness, energy efficiency and ethics.

← KM-08 lessons
Learning support only. This lesson develops workplace awareness and responsible decision-making. It does not replace employer policies, environmental procedures, legislation, supervision or formal assessment.

1. Environmental sustainability initiatives

Environmental sustainability means using resources and carrying out work in ways that reduce unnecessary harm and support long-term environmental protection. Workplace initiatives can include waste reduction, responsible material use, recycling where appropriate, spill prevention and improved housekeeping.

2. Environment and pollution

Pollution can affect air, water, soil and the wider workplace environment. Learners should be able to recognise common workplace pollution sources and explain why prevention and correct reporting matter.

3. Foundational sustainability principles

Environmental protection, conservation and sustainable development require people and organisations to consider the effects of their decisions and to use approved controls for waste, materials, emissions and resources.

Key idea: Responsible work includes considering environmental consequences, not only production output.

4. Local and global impact

A workplace activity can have immediate local effects and may also contribute to wider environmental impacts through resource use, waste and emissions. Learners should understand this connection at a basic level.
