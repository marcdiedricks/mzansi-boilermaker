The Boilermaker Trade, Learning Path, Rights and Responsibilities

Start with what the trade is, how occupational learning is structured, and what responsible participation in training and work looks like.

← KM-01 lessons
Learning only. This lesson introduces the trade and learner responsibilities. It does not authorise practical work or prove occupational competence.

1. What does a Boilermaker do?

A Boilermaker works with metal components and structures using controlled drawings, specifications, materials and workplace procedures. The trade includes planning, fabrication, forming, assembling, joining, checking and maintaining the quality of work.

Key idea: The job begins with understanding the requirement before physical work starts.

2. Occupational learning has three parts

Knowledge
What you need to understand.
Practical skills
What you learn to do in an approved training environment.
Work experience
What you learn through supervised workplace participation.

An app lesson supports knowledge learning. It does not replace practical training, workplace experience or formal assessment.

3. Your rights as a learner

A learner should receive clear learning information, appropriate supervision for practical activities, fair treatment, and access to the approved requirements that apply to the task or learning activity.

If an instruction is unclear or appears unsafe, the learner should be able to stop and ask for clarification through the responsible training or workplace process.

4. Your responsibilities as a learner

Responsible learning means following approved instructions, protecting yourself and others, using controlled information, asking when unsure, respecting workplace rules, and not pretending to be competent in tasks you have not been trained or authorised to perform.

5. Work from controlled information

Drawings, specifications, measurements, material requirements and workplace procedures must be current and approved. Memory, appearance or guesswork must not replace controlled information.

6. The learner pathway

This companion breaks the official curriculum into short lessons so that learning remains manageable on a phone. You move one Knowledge Module at a time, complete a short check, and keep local progress.

The simple learner pathway does not change the official qualification structure underneath.
