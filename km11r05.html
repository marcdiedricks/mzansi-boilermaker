Personal Hygiene, Contagious Diseases and the Workplace

Curriculum-aligned theory support for workplace fundamentals and workplace safety.

← KM-11 lessons
Safety-awareness learning only. This lesson does not provide emergency-response certification, first-aid certification, fire-fighting authorisation or permission to secure a hazardous worksite. In a real emergency, follow site procedures, alert trained adults/supervisors and use authorised emergency services.

1. Contagious-disease awareness

Contagious diseases can spread between people in different ways. Learners should understand that workplace health measures aim to reduce transmission while treating people respectfully and protecting privacy.

2. Employer and employee responsibilities

Workplaces may have duties under health-and-safety and public-health rules. Employees also have responsibilities to follow reasonable health measures and report concerns through appropriate channels.

3. Notifiable diseases

Some diseases are subject to public-health notification requirements. Learners do not need to diagnose these conditions; health professionals and authorities handle diagnosis and formal notification.

4. Personal hygiene

Good hygiene practices support health and reduce the spread of many infections. Workplace facilities, clean practices and responsible behaviour all contribute.

5. Privacy and respect

Health information can be sensitive. Learners should avoid gossip, stigma or sharing another person's health information without proper authority.

Key idea: Health protection and respect for privacy must work together.
