1. Analytical decision making

Analytical decision making uses evidence, data, critical thinking and structured problem solving to reach a reasoned conclusion.

2. A structured decision process

A useful process is to define the problem, gather relevant information, identify options, compare consequences, choose a suitable response and review the result.

3. Benefits

Analytical thinking can reduce avoidable risk, support planning, improve objectivity, encourage useful innovation and contribute to continuous improvement.

4. Limitations

Analysis is only as good as the information available. Poor data, hidden assumptions, time pressure or overconfidence can weaken a decision.

Key idea: A structured process improves decisions, but it does not make uncertainty disappear.
