Arc Welding in All Positions — Theory

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Cutting and Welding.

← KM-06 lessons
Theory only. Cutting, welding and heat-treatment processes involve serious hazards. This lesson explains concepts, process differences, quality, hazards and decision boundaries only. It does not provide operating sequences, machine settings, live-work instructions or authorisation. Practical training must be conducted in an approved environment under competent human supervision.

1. All-position concept

Welding positions describe the orientation of the joint and work. Working in different positions changes the difficulty, access and control demands on the welder.

2. Competence and training

All-position welding requires formal practical training and demonstrated competence. Reading a lesson or passing a quiz does not establish this capability.

3. Procedure control

Materials, joint design, consumables, process and acceptance criteria are controlled through approved procedures and specifications.

4. Safety boundary

Arc welding involves electrical, thermal, radiation, fume and fire hazards. Practical training must occur under qualified supervision with workplace controls.

Key idea: This lesson explains the concept only; it does not teach position-specific operating technique.
