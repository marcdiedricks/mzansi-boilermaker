PM-03 • PM-03-PS03 • PREPARATION

Cut Material Using Saws

Safe preparation and evidence support for supervised practical training.

← PM-03 activities
High-risk practical boundary. Cutting, welding, heating, powered machinery and heavy-material handling can cause serious injury. This page does not provide operating sequences, machine settings, welding parameters, gas-selection instructions, rigging methods, live-equipment checks or hands-on repair steps. Those activities require formal practical instruction and competent adult supervision.

1. Understand the task

At preparation level, identify the workpiece, required dimensions, machine category, cut-quality expectations and the need for approved machine information.

Use the approved drawing, work instruction, data sheet or training brief supplied by the provider. Separate stated requirements from assumptions, and flag anything unclear before practical work begins.

2. Recognition and hazard awareness

Identify the broad equipment category, material, task purpose, likely hazard zones, required controlled documents and the people responsible for supervision. Do not operate or adjust equipment from information in this app.

3. Quality and inspection awareness

Learn what the finished work is expected to satisfy: drawing requirements, dimensions, visible condition, traceability and documented acceptance criteria. The PWA may help organise observations; acceptance remains a human assessment function.

4. Evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare non-operational evidence such as annotated drawings, completed checklists, defect-identification worksheets, supervised-work photographs, quality records and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
