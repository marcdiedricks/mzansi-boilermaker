Hoisting, Lifting and Slinging — Theory and Safety Awareness

Curriculum-aligned knowledge support for Assembling and Joining.

← KM-05 lessons
Knowledge learning only. This lesson supports terminology, drawings, specifications, hazard recognition and planning. It does not teach or authorise welding, lifting, hoisting, slinging, powered machinery or other hazardous practical work. Practical activities require approved training, workplace controls and competent human supervision.

1. Purpose of lifting equipment

Different lifting and slinging equipment is designed for specific controlled applications and ratings. Learners should recognise categories and understand that selection for a real task requires competent workplace assessment.

2. Equipment identity and rating

Labels, certificates, inspection status and manufacturer information form part of equipment control. Damaged, unidentified or out-of-status equipment must not be assumed safe or suitable.

3. Hazard awareness

Suspended loads, pinch points, shifting loads and equipment failure can cause serious injury. The safe learning rule is to keep this module theoretical unless participating in approved supervised training.

4. Working-load calculations

The curriculum includes calculations related to working loads. The PWA may support non-operational classroom exercises, but real load determination requires the correct equipment data, configuration and competent review.

5. Human authority

The app cannot select lifting gear, approve a sling arrangement or authorise a lift. Those decisions belong to trained and authorised people using the applicable workplace procedure.

Key idea: Never let confidence from a quiz outrun workplace competence.
