Mark-Off Materials and Parts and Make Templates — Preparation

Safe preparation and evidence support for a formal practical skill activity.

← PM-01 activities
Human-supervised practical boundary. This page does not certify practical competence and does not provide operating instructions for machinery, cutting, welding, forming or other hazardous work. Real practical activity must take place under the approved provider/workplace process and competent adult supervision.

1. Purpose of marking information

At theory level, understand how controlled drawings, dimensions and reference information inform marking and template work.

2. Classroom simulation

Use paper, cardboard, supplied worksheets or digital sketches to practise recognising reference lines, dimensions and labels without performing hazardous fabrication.

3. Human-supervised practical work

Real material marking, template manufacture and any associated cutting or forming must follow the provider or workplace procedure under competent adult supervision.

Key idea: Simulation prepares understanding; it does not replace supervised trade practice.

Evidence preparation

Where your provider or workplace permits it, prepare evidence such as annotated classroom drawings, calculations, material lists, sketches, completed worksheets or photographs of supervised work. Evidence uploaded to the PWA remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
