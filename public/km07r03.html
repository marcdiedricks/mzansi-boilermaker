Scale of Drawings

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Boilermaker’s Drawings.

← KM-07 lessons
Drawing and theory learning only. This lesson develops interpretation, geometry, measurement and drawing literacy. It does not authorise fabrication, cutting, welding, lifting, machining or any other hazardous practical work.

1. What scale means

Scale expresses the relationship between the size shown on a drawing and the size of the actual object. Learners should recognise full-size, reduced and enlarged drawing concepts.

2. Reading scale information

The applicable scale should be taken from the controlled drawing information. Learners should never assume a drawing is to scale merely because it looks proportional.

3. Dimensions remain authoritative

Written dimensions and controlled notes take priority over estimating size from the picture itself.

4. Checking consistency

When a stated scale, dimension or view appears inconsistent, the correct response is to stop and clarify before relying on it.

Key idea: Never measure a printed image and assume that measurement is the job dimension.
