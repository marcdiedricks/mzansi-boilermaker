Check the Job Before Fabrication

A short quality-awareness and stop-and-check lesson within KM-04 Fabrication and Pipework.

← Main app
Learning only. This lesson explains quality checks, recognition and decision-making before practical fabrication. It does not teach cutting, welding, heating, bending, lifting, machine setup or machine operation. Practical work requires approved training, workplace controls and competent human supervision.

1. Check before work starts

Quality begins before fabrication. Confirm that the controlled drawing, dimensions, material requirements, specifications and workplace instructions agree with the job you are preparing to do.

Key idea: Finding a mismatch before practical work starts can prevent waste, rework and unsafe decisions.

2. Confirm the important details

Check that the required component, material, size, thickness, grade and identification match the controlled job information. Do not rely on memory or appearance alone.

3. Notice mismatches and uncertainty

A missing dimension, unclear note, damaged identification mark or difference between the material and the job information is a reason to stop and clarify. Do not quietly substitute, guess or continue.

4. Quality is not only the final inspection

Quality awareness is part of planning. Checking information and material before practical work helps reduce avoidable mistakes and supports traceability.

5. Know when to stop and ask

If controlled information is incomplete, contradictory or unclear, stop and ask the responsible supervisor or training professional. A learner should not resolve an important uncertainty by guessing.
