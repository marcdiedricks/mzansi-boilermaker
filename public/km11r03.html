Basic First-Aid Awareness and Escalation

Curriculum-aligned theory support for workplace fundamentals and workplace safety.

← KM-11 lessons
Safety-awareness learning only. This lesson does not provide emergency-response certification, first-aid certification, fire-fighting authorisation or permission to secure a hazardous worksite. In a real emergency, follow site procedures, alert trained adults/supervisors and use authorised emergency services.

1. Purpose of first-aid awareness

First-aid awareness helps a learner recognise that an injury or sudden illness may require urgent help. Formal first-aid treatment should be provided by appropriately trained people according to workplace procedures.

2. Protecting the scene

Before approaching an injured person, learners should avoid placing themselves or others in additional danger and should alert the responsible supervisor or emergency system.

3. Common emergency categories

The curriculum refers to bleeding, burns, electrical injury, loss of consciousness and seizure-type events. At learner level, the priority is recognition, calling for trained help and following the site's emergency process.

4. CPR and specialist actions

CPR and other hands-on emergency techniques require proper practical training and current recognised guidance. This PWA does not teach step-by-step CPR or substitute for certified training.

Key idea: Know when to get help; do not let app-based knowledge create false confidence in an emergency.
