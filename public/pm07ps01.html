Maintain Safe Work Environment — Recognition and Reporting

Safety and housekeeping preparation for supervised practical learning.

← PM-07 activities
Safety boundary. This page supports recognition, checklist use, reporting and housekeeping awareness only. It does not teach hazardous-substance handling procedures, machine testing, emergency-stop testing, lifting methods, PPE selection for specific hazards or other live safety-critical actions. Follow approved site procedures and competent adult supervision.

1. Understand the activity

Recognise workplace hazards, the purpose of approved PPE/PPC, safety signs, alarms, clean-work-area expectations and the need to report unsafe conditions. The official detailed curriculum contains some generic wording that appears inherited from a broader template; the app keeps only trade-relevant, safety-safe support and flags source anomalies for final audit.

2. Safety-control awareness

Learn to recognise the purpose of PPE/PPC, signs, alarms, guards, emergency controls, clean work areas and hazard reporting. The site or provider determines the approved controls for each real activity.

3. Housekeeping and reporting

Good housekeeping supports visibility, access and safer work. Learners may practise identifying clutter, waste, spills, misplaced items or unsafe conditions in simulations and report them through the approved process rather than taking unsafe corrective action.

4. Evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare evidence such as completed housekeeping checklists, safety-observation forms, simulated inspection records and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
