Use Communication Channels and Processes

Communication and workplace-interaction preparation for supervised practical learning.

← PM-08 activities
Human-process boundary. This page supports communication practice, reflection and evidence preparation only. It does not replace supervisors, facilitators, workplace policies, formal grievance/disciplinary processes or professional support.

1. Understand the activity

Use case studies, task instructions, approved communication protocols and simulated workplace situations to practise selecting a suitable communication tool, participating in a meeting/reporting process and demonstrating active listening.

2. Clear communication

Practise giving and receiving information clearly, using the correct channel for the task, checking understanding and keeping required records. If instructions are unclear or conflict with safety requirements, stop and ask the responsible person.

3. Listening and respectful interaction

Active listening includes paying attention, asking clarifying questions and repeating key information back in your own words. Respectful communication does not require accepting unsafe, abusive or unlawful behaviour.

4. Evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare non-sensitive evidence such as meeting notes, simulated reporting forms, communication exercises, reflective notes and facilitator feedback. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
