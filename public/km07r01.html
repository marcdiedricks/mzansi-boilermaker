Plate, Pipe and Structural Steel Drawing Concepts and Terminology

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Boilermaker’s Drawings.

← KM-07 lessons
Drawing and theory learning only. This lesson develops interpretation, geometry, measurement and drawing literacy. It does not authorise fabrication, cutting, welding, lifting, machining or any other hazardous practical work.

1. Drawing purpose

Engineering drawings communicate controlled information about components, structures and assemblies. Learners should understand the difference between a sketch, a formal drawing and supporting specifications.

2. Trade terminology

Boilermaker drawings use technical terms for plate, pipe, sections, views, dimensions, notes and references. Correct terminology supports accurate communication between learners, facilitators and workplaces.

3. Controlled information

A drawing must be identified by its title, number, revision and other control information where provided. Learners should not rely on an unverified or outdated copy.

Key idea: The current approved drawing is the reference, not memory.

4. Drawing families

Plate, pipe and structural steel drawings may emphasise different features, but all require careful reading of views, dimensions, notes and symbols.
