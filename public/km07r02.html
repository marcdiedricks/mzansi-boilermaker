Basic Geometry and Units of Measurement

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Boilermaker’s Drawings.

← KM-07 lessons
Drawing and theory learning only. This lesson develops interpretation, geometry, measurement and drawing literacy. It does not authorise fabrication, cutting, welding, lifting, machining or any other hazardous practical work.

1. Geometry in fabrication drawings

Basic geometry helps learners understand shapes, angles, circles, triangles, rectangles and relationships between features shown on drawings.

2. Units

Drawings use defined units of measurement. Learners should identify the unit system stated on the drawing and keep calculations consistent.

3. Accuracy

Small measurement errors can become large fabrication errors. Learners should read values carefully and avoid mixing units without deliberate conversion.

4. Calculations as learning

The PWA may support classroom-style geometry and unit-conversion practice. These calculations support understanding but do not authorise practical fabrication.

Key idea: Correct units are part of a correct answer.
