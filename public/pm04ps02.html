PM-04 • PM-04-PS02 • PREPARATION

Form Material Using Benders

Safe preparation and evidence support for supervised practical training.

← PM-04 activities
High-risk practical boundary. Rolling, bending, forming, heating and fabrication can create crush, pinch, burn and machinery hazards. This page does not provide machine settings, forming sequences, heating procedures, operating instructions or live fabrication methods. Real practical activity requires approved equipment, workplace procedures and competent adult supervision.

1. Understand the task

Recognise the required shape, material/profile information, drawing dimensions, bend-related references and quality expectations without providing machine-operation or bending instructions.

Use the approved drawing, task specification and training brief supplied by the provider. Separate stated requirements from assumptions and flag anything unclear before practical work begins.

2. Material and limitation awareness

At preparation level, identify the material type, section or profile, dimensions, drawing references and any stated limitations. Do not infer bending limits, heat requirements or machine capability from appearance alone.

3. Hazard and control awareness

Recognise broad hazard categories such as moving machinery, pinch/crush zones, heated material, unstable workpieces, sharp edges and handling risks. Learn the stop-and-escalate boundary rather than attempting the process independently.

4. Quality and evidence preparation

Where permitted, prepare non-operational evidence such as annotated drawings, classroom calculations, dimensional-check sheets, supervised-work photographs, quality observations and reflective notes. Evidence remains not verified until reviewed by an authorised human.
