Employment and Employer–Employee Relationships

Curriculum-aligned theory support for workplace fundamentals and workplace safety.

← KM-11 lessons
Safety-awareness learning only. This lesson does not provide emergency-response certification, first-aid certification, fire-fighting authorisation or permission to secure a hazardous worksite. In a real emergency, follow site procedures, alert trained adults/supervisors and use authorised emergency services.

1. Employment and workplace rules

Employment relationships are shaped by applicable law, workplace policies, contracts and agreed responsibilities. Learners should understand that employers and employees both have duties and rights.

2. Organised labour

Trade unions and other organised-labour structures can represent workers and participate in workplace consultation, negotiation and dispute processes.

3. Employment and learning contracts

Employment contracts, apprenticeships, learnerships and other learning agreements define different relationships and responsibilities. Learners should read and understand the terms that apply to them.

4. Values, codes and ethics

Organisations often define values, rules, codes of conduct and ethical expectations. These guide behaviour alongside legal duties and workplace procedures.

5. Labour-relations processes

Discipline, grievances, negotiation and dispute-resolution processes should follow lawful and organisational procedures rather than personal confrontation.

Key idea: When a workplace issue is serious or unclear, use the recognised process and seek appropriate guidance.
