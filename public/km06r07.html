Oxy-Fuel Cutting — Theory and Hazard Awareness

Curriculum-aligned theory support for Cutting and Welding.

← KM-06 lessons
Theory only. Cutting, welding and heat-treatment processes involve serious hazards. This lesson explains concepts, process differences, quality, hazards and decision boundaries only. It does not provide operating sequences, machine settings, live-work instructions or authorisation. Practical training must be conducted in an approved environment under competent human supervision.

1. What oxy-fuel cutting is

Oxy-fuel cutting is a thermal cutting process used in suitable applications. Learners should recognise the process, its purpose and its limitations without attempting to set up or operate the equipment.

2. Suitability and material considerations

The process is not suitable for every material or job. Approved procedures, material type, thickness, quality requirements and workplace controls determine applicability.

3. Hazard awareness

Oxy-fuel systems involve fuel gas, oxygen, flame, hot metal and fire hazards. Equipment condition, storage and operation require trained personnel and formal workplace procedures.

4. Quality awareness

Cut quality is evaluated against job requirements such as dimensions, edge condition and downstream fabrication needs. Learners should know that acceptance is controlled by the specification and inspection process.

Key idea: No gas-handling or ignition instructions are provided in this learning companion.
